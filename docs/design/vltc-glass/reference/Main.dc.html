How we check a deal

Read the full rules
History

We judge a deal after 14 days of prices.

Before that it says "Not enough history yet".

Deal to avoid

The price or was price rose more than 3% in the last 14 days.

Or the was price is higher than any price charged in 30 days.

Good deal

At least 5% below its lowest price of the previous 30 days.

And the was price was not raised in that time.

Cheaper elsewhere

The same product is at least 2% cheaper at another shop today.

Only for exact matches by barcode or part number.
